01 / Our understanding of business growth

Every business reaches a stage where the challenges change.

What worked when the business was small may not work when the business begins to scale.

The founder who once managed everything personally may now need a management structure.

A sales process that worked at ₹1 Crore may not work at ₹10 Crore.

An informal operation may need systems.

A successful local business may need a completely different architecture before expanding nationally.

And a business looking for investment needs to be able to withstand investor scrutiny.
